Pilgrim: The Narrow Way

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · Applies to the Android app and the web version

The short version: the game does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no internet connection needed to play.

Who we are

Pilgrim: The Narrow Way is a story adventure for kids and families based on John Bunyan's The Pilgrim's Progress (1678). It is made and published by Stalin Pusparaj ("we", "us").

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect or transmit personal data of any kind. It does not request your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Data stored on your device

To let you continue your journey, the game keeps your save slots and your settings (sound volume, text size, language, controls) only on your own device, in the app's private storage. This data never leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can also clear it from the game's settings or from your device's app settings.

Internet access

Everything in the game, including every model, sound and tune, is generated by the app itself. The Android app plays fully offline and does not request network permission. The web version loads from our site and makes no further requests once it has loaded.

Children's privacy

The game is designed for children aged 6 to 12 and their families. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and Google Play's Families policies. There are no in-app purchases, advertisements, social features, chat or external links inside the game.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks or analytics services. The Android app is distributed through Google Play, whose own privacy policy covers your download and installation.

Permissions

The Android app requests no permissions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
