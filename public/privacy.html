Privacy Policy

Pilgrim: The Narrow Way
Developed by GEM CREATORS
Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Pilgrim: The Narrow Way is a browser-based 3D story adventure game for kids and families. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all users, especially children.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account registration is required.
  • No names, email addresses, or contact information are collected.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs are collected.
  • No usage data or analytics are sent to our servers.

Local Storage

Game progress (save data, settings) is stored locally on your device using your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

No Ads

This game contains no advertisements. No ad networks, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics are included.

No In-App Purchases

The free demo (Chapters I–IV) contains no in-app purchases. The full game is available as a one-time purchase with no ongoing charges.

Children's Privacy

Pilgrim: The Narrow Way is designed for users aged 6 and older. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). Because we collect no personal data at all, no parental consent is required for children to use this app.

Third-Party Services

The game runs entirely in your browser and does not communicate with any third-party servers during gameplay. The only network request is the initial download of the game files from our hosting provider (Netlify).

Permissions

The Android version of this app requires no special permissions beyond internet access (to load the game).

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will update the "last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
