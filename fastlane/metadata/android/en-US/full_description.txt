A little pilgrim with a big burden walks the narrow way to the Celestial City.

Pilgrim: The Narrow Way is a gentle 3D Bible adventure game for kids and families, based on John Bunyan's classic Christian story The Pilgrim's Progress (1678). Children walk the whole journey themselves: through the City of Destruction, the Slough of Despond, the Wicket Gate, the Cross, Vanity Fair, Doubting Castle and the Delectable Mountains, all the way to the River and the Celestial City.

★ A STORY ADVENTURE KIDS CAN PLAY

• Simple touch controls: hold your thumb to walk the Way, drag to explore
• Meet Christian's friends and foes: Evangelist, Pliable, Faithful, Hopeful, Giant Despair and more
• Gentle puzzles, mini-games, a friendly boss battle with Apollyon, and a laugh in every chapter
• No deaths: if you fall, you bounce back and keep going
