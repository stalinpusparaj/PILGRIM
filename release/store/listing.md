Full description:
A little pilgrim with a big burden walks the narrow way to the Celestial City.

PILGRIM: THE NARROW WAY is a gentle 3D story adventure for kids aged 6–12 and their families, based on John Bunyan's classic The Pilgrim's Progress (1678). Explore a colourful toy-box world, meet friends like Evangelist, Pliable and Help, solve small puzzles, and keep your lantern of Hope burning bright.
